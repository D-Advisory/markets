Data sources

Every number on this page comes from a free public source. Market prices are Yahoo Finance quotes (Canadian stocks in CAD). U.S. rates, inflation and jobs come from FRED, the St. Louis Fed's database, which republishes the official Treasury, BLS, BEA, Federal Reserve and Freddie Mac releases. Canadian rates and CPI come straight from the Bank of Canada's Valet data service; the unemployment rate and 3-month rate come from Statistics Canada and the OECD via FRED.

Weekly points in the charts are each Friday's last available close. Daily changes on tiles are versus the prior session. For information only; not investment advice.
